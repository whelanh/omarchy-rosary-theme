# Credits and licensing

## The paintings

The twenty paintings in `art/originals/` were downloaded from **The Free
Catholic Gallery** at [sdcason.com](https://sdcason.com/), run by **Shalone
Cason**. That site licenses the images it posts for free under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The underlying works are public domain, but the CC BY 4.0 terms apply to the
files as supplied by the gallery. Attribution is therefore required.

### Changes made

For this theme the source images were cropped (mostly to remove scanner or
paper edges), colour- and contrast-adjusted, blurred and darkened to form a
backdrop, placed in a gold frame, given a caption, and resized to 3440×1440.
The originals in `art/originals/` are the unmodified downloaded files. The
wallpapers in `paintings/` are the adapted versions. The cursor theme, the
crucifix badge and the login chime are original work, not derived from the
paintings.
