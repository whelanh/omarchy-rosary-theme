# Credits and licensing

## The paintings

Nineteen of the twenty paintings in `art/originals/` were downloaded from
**The Free Catholic Gallery** at [sdcason.com](https://sdcason.com/), run by
**Shalone Cason**. That site licenses the images it posts for free under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The underlying works are public domain, and some individual gallery pages say
so; the gallery's blanket licence is CC BY 4.0, so the images are credited
under that licence here. Attribution is therefore required.

**The Finding in the Temple** was downloaded from the
[Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436672),
whose open-access image is public domain / CC0. It is credited to the Met and
to Abraham Hondius below.

### Changes made

For this theme the source images were cropped (mostly to remove scanner or
paper edges), colour- and contrast-adjusted, blurred and darkened to form a
backdrop, placed in a gold frame, given a caption, and resized to 3440×1440.
The originals in `art/originals/` are the unmodified downloaded files. The
wallpapers in `paintings/` are the adapted versions. The cursor theme, the
crucifix badge and the login chime are original work, not derived from the
paintings.

## Audio

The login chime (`extras/login.wav`) is trimmed from **G20-12-Mission Bells**
by **craigsmith** on Freesound, released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Source: <https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/438227/>

The spoken slate at the start was removed, the first four actual bell tolls
were kept, and the clip was faded and levelled. The source recording is
`art/originals/mission-bells.mp3`.
